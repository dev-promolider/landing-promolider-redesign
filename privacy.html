PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Promolider LLC ("we", "us", "Company") values your privacy. When you visit www.promolider.org, register an account, or purchase services, we collect personal information including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Account Credentials: Usernames, encrypted passwords, and profile details.
  • Transaction & Billing Data: Payment information processed securely via authorized third-party payment processors (e.g., Stripe, Mercury). We do not store full credit card details on our servers.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, device information, and usage logs via cookies and standard web analytics.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We collect and use your personal information for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our EdTech platform and digital marketplace;
  • To process transactions, billing, and distribute affiliate revenue share/commissions;
  • To send administrative updates, account confirmations, and customer support messages;
  • To comply with legal obligations, anti-money laundering (AML) regulations, and financial audit standards;
  • To prevent fraud and secure our software infrastructure.

3. DATA SHARING AND THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURES

Promolider LLC does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only with trusted service providers strictly necessary to execute our platform operations:

  • Payment Processors and Banking Partners (for secure payment processing and payout settlement);
  • Cloud Hosting & Infrastructure Providers (AWS / secure servers);
  • Compliance and Legal Authorities (if required by valid legal subpoena, court order, or regulatory body).

4. DATA SECURITY AND RETENTION

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures (including SSL encryption and access controls) to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill business services and comply with financial record-keeping laws.

5. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, update, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact our support team at informacion@promolider.org.
